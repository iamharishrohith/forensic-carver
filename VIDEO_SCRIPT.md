# 🎬 ACPIA: Scene-by-Scene AI Video Generation Script & Storyboard

This storyboard is designed for AI video generators (e.g., **Sora**, **Runway Gen-2**, or **Pika Labs**) to produce a high-impact B-roll video, combined with a voiceover generated by **ElevenLabs** or **HeyGen**.

---

### **Video Settings & Tone**
*   **Visual Style:** Cinematic, technical, dark-mode, high-contrast, cyberpunk-influenced slate blue/purple accent lights, dramatic lighting.
*   **Audio/Music Cues:** Low, pulsing electronic bass, building in intensity to match the progression of technology.
*   **Overall Vibe:** High-stakes, professional, cutting-edge, secure.

---

## 🎞️ Storyboard Breakdown

### **Scene 1: The Inundation (0:00 - 0:20)**
*   **Voiceover:** 
    > *"Every day, child protection investigators are drowning in a tsunami of digital evidence. A single case can involve terabytes of chat logs, device dumps, and geotagged files. The result? Severe backlogs while children remain at risk. But standard cloud-based AI tools are a legal and ethical dead end due to victim confidentiality regulations. That is why we built ACPIA."*
*   **Visual Scene (B-Roll Prompt):**
    > `Close up of an investigator in a dark room, face lit by the cold glow of multiple monitor screens showing columns of raw code and directory trees. Camera slowly pans around to show shelves of external hard drives and seized smartphones. High-contrast, cinematic, 4k.`
