# ⏱️ ACPIA: 2-Minute Project Pitch Script

**Word Count:** ~280 words  
**Target Time:** 120 seconds (delivered at a steady, engaging pace)

---

### **[0:00 - 0:20] The Hook & The Problem**
> *"Every day, child protection investigators are drowning in a tsunami of digital evidence. A single case can involve terabytes of chat logs, device dumps, and geotagged files. The result? Severe backlogs while children remain at risk. But the standard solution—sending this data to the cloud for AI analysis—is a legal and ethical dead end. Victim privacy regulations and strict evidence security demand that forensic labs operate 100% offline."*

---

### **[0:20 - 0:50] The Solution (The ACPIA Copilot)**
> *"This is why we built **ACPIA**—the Agentic Child Protection Investigation Assistant. ACPIA is an offline-first, air-gapped AI copilot designed to work directly inside secure forensic labs. It doesn’t send data to the cloud. Instead, it runs a high-performance, tiered AI pipeline directly on local workstations."*

---

### **[0:50 - 1:30] How It Works (The Technology)**
> *"When evidence is loaded, a lightning-fast **Tier 1 triage classifier** scans text in under 5 milliseconds. If coercion or threats are flagged, it escalates to **Tier 2: deep CPU-bound NLP models** that identify grooming patterns and extract key actors. 
> 
> A collaborative team of **9 autonomous agents** then takes over: mining relationships, plotting geotagged movement timelines, scanning local OSINT records like Truecaller details, and even identifying if a device or phone number has appeared in separate historical cases."*

---

### **[1:30 - 2:00] Forensic Rigor & Close**
> *"Crucially, ACPIA is built for the courtroom. Through a **Human-in-the-Loop** model, every AI finding is verified by an investigator. The system maintains an immutable audit ledger protected by SHA-256 signatures, producing a court-ready PDF report with a bulletproof chain of custody. 
> 
> ACPIA turns days of manual tracking into seconds of automated intelligence—protecting victims, empowering cops, and keeping communities safe. Thank you."*
